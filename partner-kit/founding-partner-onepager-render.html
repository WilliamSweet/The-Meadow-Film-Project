Become a Founding Partner

A native-landscape documentary — releasing September 2026

Over 40 million acres of American lawn — more than cotton, rice, potatoes, and every fruit, nut, and berry orchard in the country combined. This film is a portrait of the Americans who turned it back into something alive, and of the movement making that the new normal.

We are assembling a founding coalition of the organizations who built this movement — named on record, before the film reaches the public.

What a Founding Partner receives

Named in the film as a Founding Partner. Your organization appears in the credits of the finished documentary. This is the anchor — it exists nowhere else, and it does not reopen after the founding window closes.

A permanent place on the coalition page. Your logo, a one-line description of your work, and a link back to your site — listed alongside Homegrown National Park, Doug Tallamy, the Pollinator Pathway, and the University of Connecticut. Real traffic, real association.

A "Founding Partner" badge. A finished graphic (below) to display on your own website, newsletter, and social channels — the mark of an organization that helped start this.

First look, before the public. An invitation to a private screening of the film ahead of its public release.

A co-branded screening kit. After launch, everything you need to host your own screening for your community — so the film reaches your audience in your voice, not just ours.

What we ask of you

  • Register before the founding window closes — July 13, 2026.
  • Send your logo, a one-line description, and a link.
  • When the film launches, share it once with your audience. We hand you the assets; the effort is ours, the credit is yours.

That is the whole ask. No dues, no meetings, no standing commitment.

How it works

  1. Register at meadowfilm.studio → Become a Founding Partner.
  2. We review and add your organization to the coalition page.
  3. You receive your badge and partner kit by email.
  4. At launch, you get the share kit — one post, ready to go.
